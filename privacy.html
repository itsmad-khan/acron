Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

💡 Simple version: We collect only what we need to run your account. We never sell your data. Your data is safe with Google Firebase.

1. Who We Are

Acron is a free educational platform for Pakistani students. We are based in Pakistan and our website is available at acron-psi.vercel.app

2. What Information We Collect

📧 Email address — Used for account creation and login only
👤 Name — Used to personalize your dashboard greeting
🏫 Board and class — Used to show you the right books and subjects
📊 Quiz history — Saved so you can track your progress over time
📄 PDF files — PDFs you upload for quiz are processed temporarily and NOT stored on our servers

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Show you your board's books and subjects
  • Save your quiz history and progress
  • Send you email verification and account notices
  • Improve our platform based on usage patterns

4. We Never Sell Your Data

We never sell, trade or share your personal information with any third party for commercial purposes. Your data belongs to you.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase — one of the world's most trusted and secure cloud platforms.

✅ All data is encrypted
✅ Passwords are hashed — we cannot see them
✅ Each user can only access their own data
✅ Data is backed up automatically by Google

6. Cookies

We use browser localStorage to keep you logged in and remember your preferences like language and theme. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies.

7. Third Party Services

We use these trusted third party services:

🔥 Google Firebase — Authentication and database
▲ Vercel — Website hosting
🤖 GitHub AI Models — Quiz generation
📁 Google Drive — Book storage

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:
  • Access your personal data anytime from your profile page
  • Update your information from your profile page
  • Delete your account and all data from your profile page
  • Contact us about any privacy concerns

9. Children's Privacy

Acron is designed for students of all ages including children. We take children's privacy very seriously. We collect minimum information necessary and never share it with anyone.

10. Contact Us About Privacy

If you have any questions about this privacy policy please contact us:
